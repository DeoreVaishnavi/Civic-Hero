# Citizen Training Guide

## Learning outcomes
A citizen should be able to register, report an issue, track it, verify the resolution and use rewards safely.

## Demonstration
1. Register and verify email.
2. Open **Report Issue**.
3. Choose category, department and ward.
4. Describe the issue clearly.
5. Capture GPS and add evidence images.
6. Submit and save the complaint reference.
7. Track status through **My Complaints**.
8. Review resolution evidence when verification is requested.
9. Approve with a rating or reject with a clear reason.
10. Review notifications, points, badges and the chatbot.

## Safety
Do not upload private documents, unrelated people, passwords or sensitive personal information. Report genuine civic issues only.
