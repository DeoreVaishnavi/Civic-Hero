# Officer Training Guide

## Learning outcomes
An officer should manage assigned complaints, maintain an auditable progress record and submit reliable resolution evidence.

## Workflow
1. Open the work queue and review SLA priority.
2. Accept the assignment or reject it with a valid operational reason.
3. Review complaint location, citizen evidence and timeline.
4. Record progress updates from the field.
5. Add GPS when available.
6. Upload clear before/after resolution evidence.
7. Enter accurate resolution notes.
8. Submit the complaint for citizen verification.

## Quality rules
Never close work without evidence. Never expose citizen contact data outside authorised use. Record delays before SLA expiry.
