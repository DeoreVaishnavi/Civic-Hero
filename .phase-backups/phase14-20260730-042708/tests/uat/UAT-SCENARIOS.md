# CivicHero User Acceptance Test Scenarios

## Citizen
1. Register, verify email and log in.
2. Submit a geo-tagged complaint with evidence.
3. Track timeline, receive notifications and upvote a nearby issue.
4. Approve a genuine resolution and confirm reward points.
5. Reject an unsatisfactory resolution and raise a dispute.

## Officer
1. View assigned workload and SLA state.
2. Accept an assignment and add a field progress update.
3. Upload resolution evidence and submit for citizen verification.

## Supervisor
1. Assign an unassigned complaint using workload information.
2. Reassign a rejected or overdue complaint.
3. Review disputes, order rework and monitor SLA performance.

## Admin / SuperAdmin
1. Manage users, roles, departments, wards and categories.
2. Review AI triage, analytics, security events and audit logs.
3. Send a broadcast notification and verify real-time delivery.
4. Review system health, quality readiness and maintenance controls.

## Acceptance criteria
- At least 95% of executed UAT scenarios pass.
- No Critical or Major defect remains open.
- Role isolation, data ownership and audit logging are verified.
- Stakeholder sign-off is recorded in `UAT-SIGNOFF-TEMPLATE.md`.
