# Departments and their responsibilities in the civic hero system

## Public Works Department
- Road maintenance and repairs
- Public utilities (water, sewer, storm drains)
- Street lighting and traffic signals
- Parks and recreation facilities maintenance
- Solid waste management and recycling

## Public Safety Department
- Police services and law enforcement
- Fire prevention and emergency response
- Emergency medical services (EMS)
- Traffic safety and enforcement
- Disaster preparedness and response

## Community Services Department
- Parks and recreation programs
- Library services
- Senior services and programs
- Youth and family services
- Community events and festivals
- Volunteer coordination

## Community Development Department
- Building permits and inspections
- Planning and zoning
- Code enforcement
- Housing assistance programs
- Economic development initiatives
- Business licensing and permits

## Finance Department
- Budget preparation and management
- Tax collection and processing
- Payroll and employee benefits
- Accounts payable and receivable
- Financial reporting and auditing
- Treasury and investment management

## Human Resources Department
- Recruitment and hiring
- Employee training and development
- Performance management
- Employee relations and conflict resolution
- Benefits administration
- Payroll processing
- Labor relations and compliance

## City Manager's Office
- Overall city administration and management
- Policy development and implementation
- Interdepartmental coordination
- Public information and communications
- Legislative affairs and council support
- Special projects and initiatives

## City Attorney's Office
- Legal counsel and advice
- Litigation representation
- Contract review and drafting
- Ordinance preparation
- Legal compliance and risk management
- Public records requests

## City Clerk's Office
- Official records management
- Meeting minutes and agendas
- Election administration
- Public notices and postings
- Licenses and permits processing
- Archives and historical records
