# Administrator Training Guide

## Administration areas
- Users, roles and scope.
- Categories, departments and wards.
- Broadcast notifications.
- Analytics and exports.
- AI review queue.
- Audit and security centres.
- Quality, release and launch readiness.

## Protected actions
Use SuperAdmin only for high-risk operations such as managing another SuperAdmin, protected cleanup or final system-level decisions.

## Launch responsibility
Administrators must distinguish **configured**, **tested**, **approved** and **deployed**. A prepared checklist is not test evidence, and a template is not stakeholder sign-off.
