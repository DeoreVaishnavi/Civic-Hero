# Common Complaint Categories in CivicHero System

## Service Quality Issues
- Delayed response times
- Incomplete or incorrect service delivery
- Poor quality of work performed
- Failure to follow up on service requests
- Inconsistent service levels across different areas

## Staff Conduct and Behavior
- Rude or unprofessional behavior by staff
- Lack of courtesy or respect
- Discrimination or harassment
- Failure to identify oneself properly
- Inappropriate use of authority

## Facility and Infrastructure Problems
- Potholes and road damage
- Broken street lights or traffic signals
- Park maintenance issues (broken equipment, unsafe conditions)
- Water leaks or sewage problems
- Building maintenance issues (city facilities)
- Graffiti and vandalism

## Communication Problems
- Failure to return phone calls or emails
- Lack of updates on ongoing issues
- Conflicting information from different staff members
- Difficulty reaching the right department or person
- Inadequate public notification about projects or changes

## Policy and Procedure Concerns
- Unclear or confusing procedures
- Inconsistent application of rules
- Excessive bureaucracy or red tape
- Lack of transparency in decision-making
- Failure to follow established protocols

## Financial and Billing Issues
- Incorrect utility billing
- Unexpected fees or charges
- Disputes over fines or penalties
- Refund processing delays
- Issues with payment processing

## Safety and Security Concerns
- Hazardous conditions on public property
- Inadequate lighting in public areas
- Security concerns in parks or facilities
- Traffic safety issues
- Emergency response delays

## Environmental Issues
- Illegal dumping or littering
- Pollution concerns (air, water, noise)
- Improper waste disposal
- Pest infestations in public areas
- Tree maintenance or removal concerns

## Accessibility and Accommodation
- Lack of accessibility for people with disabilities
- Failure to provide reasonable accommodations
- Barriers to accessing city services or facilities
- Inadequate signage or wayfinding
- Issues with public transportation accessibility
