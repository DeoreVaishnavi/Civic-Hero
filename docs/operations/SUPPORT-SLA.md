# CivicHero Support SLA

| Severity | Acknowledge | Target response |
|---|---:|---:|
| Critical | 15 minutes | Continuous response until containment |
| Major | 1 hour | Same business day mitigation |
| Minor | 1 business day | Planned maintenance release |
| Request | 2 business days | Product backlog decision |

These are operational targets, not contractual guarantees. Municipal stakeholders should approve the final support policy.
