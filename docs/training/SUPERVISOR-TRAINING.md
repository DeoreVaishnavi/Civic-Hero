# Supervisor Training Guide

## Responsibilities
- Balance officer workload.
- Assign within department and ward scope.
- Monitor SLA and overdue cases.
- Review rejections, disputes and verification delays.
- Escalate material operational risks.

## Daily routine
1. Review unassigned and critical complaints.
2. Check eligible-officer workload.
3. Assign or bulk assign work.
4. Review overdue assignments and stalled progress.
5. Handle dispute evidence impartially.
6. Order rework when resolution quality is insufficient.
7. Review department analytics and satisfaction.
