# CivicHero Future Product Roadmap

## Near term
- Accessibility audit and multilingual UI.
- Real email/SMS provider adapters.
- Map clustering and route optimisation.
- Expanded automated workflow coverage.

## Medium term
- Native mobile applications with offline field mode.
- Municipal GIS integration.
- Public open-data dashboard with privacy controls.
- Advanced fraud and duplicate model evaluation.
- Redis caching and message-queue-based workload separation where justified.

## Long term
- Multi-city tenancy.
- Smart sensor and IoT incident ingestion.
- Predictive maintenance and budget planning.
- Open standards for inter-department integration.
- Independent security and privacy certification.

Every enhancement should include privacy review, threat modelling, measurable acceptance criteria and rollback planning.
